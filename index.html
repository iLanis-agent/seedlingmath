Seedling math

The seed packet promises plenty, half the cells stay empty, and the lettuce all bolts in the same week. Seedling math turns your plant list into seeds to sow at a labeled germination rate, counts the days to the last frost against the head start on the packet, and splits the season into sowing batches so the harvest arrives in weeks instead of all at once. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Count plan - plants wanted and germination rate become seeds to sow and spare seedlings.
  • Calendar plan - days to the last frost and the packet's head start become the sowing countdown.
  • Batch plan - a harvest window and sowing interval become batches of plants.

Labeled norms

Labeled germination: 80% for fresh tomato seed, 65% for older packets - illustrative, since seed age and soil warmth move the number. Labeled head starts: 6 weeks for tomatoes, 8 for peppers. The seed packet's own instructions and your last frost date win.
